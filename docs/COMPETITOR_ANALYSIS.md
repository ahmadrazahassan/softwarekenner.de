# Competitor analysis (21 October 2026)

Short look at comparable German review sites to sharpen our own pages. We describe structure only; no content was copied.

## OMR Reviews (omr.com/de/reviews, category Buchhaltung)

- **Structure:** H1, filter bar ("Filtern (174 Produkte)"), sorting by OMR Score, rating, review count or name, company size filter (Kleinunternehmen, Mittelstand, Enterprise), product list with 20 cards per page, long explanatory text, content hub.
- **Cards:** logo, name, badges such as "Leader" or "Top Rated" per quarter, "Verifiziertes Profil", star rating with review count, "Preis: Ab …", short description, CTA ("Kostenlos testen", "Live-Demo buchen"), feature tags.
- **Ranking:** OMR Score from average rating, review count and recency. Product cards carry partner tracking without a visible "Anzeige" label; only content hub articles are marked "Sponsored".
- **Top of the list (snapshot):** BuchhaltungsButler, orgaMAX, GetMyInvoices, WISO MeinBüro, sevdesk, Holvi, BillingEngine, easybill, FastBill, Lexware Office, MOCO, Shine, DATEV, Accountable, finway.

## Für-Gründer.de (Buchhaltungssoftware-Vergleich 2026)

- **Structure:** intro, named author and reviewer box, affiliate notice, "Das Wichtigste auf einen Blick", top 3 boxes with grade, table with sub grades, 141 criteria in 6 groups, live test procedure in five steps, detailed profiles, exclusion list with reasons, large comparison table, FAQ, PDF download.
- **Grades:** sevdesk 1,1, Lexware Office 1,2, WISO MeinBüro 1,4, Papierkram 1,8, orgaMAX 2,0, BuchhaltungsButler 2,2, Accountable 2,4, FastBill 2,7, Sage Active 2,8, Kontolino! 3,0, Collmex 3,2, FreeFIBU 3,8.
- **Credibility:** own live test with trial accounts and named authors. Weakness: some grades differ between the overview table and the individual texts.

## Capterra, trusted.de

Both blocked automated access from our research environment (HTTP 403). Not analysed.

## What we take from this

1. **Honest labels win trust.** OMR does not mark partner links on cards; we keep "Anzeige" and "*" on every affiliate link (Section 18) and say so on the category page.
2. **Show the method next to the ranking.** Für-Gründer explains criteria and process prominently. Our MethodologyBar and /methodik do this; we add the editorial note "So ist diese Bewertung entstanden" to every review.
3. **Do not claim tests we have not run.** Für-Gründer's strength is the live test. Until we have test accounts, our reviews say "im Überblick" and name their sources; scores are drafts for the owner's review.
4. **Facts beat adjectives.** Every review carries sourced key figures (Kennzahlen) and charts built from our own price data (Preisvergleich, Kostenvergleich).
5. **Company size and Germany matter.** OMR filters by company size; we filter by size, server location, compliance and German vendor.
6. **Consistency.** One score per product and category, computed from the same data on every page, so tables and texts cannot contradict each other.
